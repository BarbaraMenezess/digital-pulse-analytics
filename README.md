## 📌 Overview

**Digital Pulse Analytics** is an interactive platform built to analyze the relationship between the growth of the digital influencer market and the stability/resilience of regional GDP across Brazilian states from 2023 to 2024.

The core objective is to identify whether regions with higher revenue density in the creative economy exhibit stronger capabilities in buffering economic downturns or fluctuations in traditional GDP.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

- **Executive KPI Overview**: Real-time tracking of average GDP variation, total accumulated digital revenue, and digital resilience months.
- **Dual-Axis Time Series Analysis**: Integrated visualization contrasting macroeconomic GDP trends against influencer market growth.
- **Quadrant Mapping**: Interactive scatter plot identifying state-level economic expansion versus downturn behavior.
- **Dynamic Filters**: Seamless filtering by year and state/region.
- **Executive Design System**: Sophisticated palette featuring navy blue (`#1E3A8A`), emerald accents, and an off-white background (`#FDFBF7`).
